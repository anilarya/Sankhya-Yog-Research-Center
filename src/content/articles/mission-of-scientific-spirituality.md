In this mission essay, Dr. Harish Chandra proposes a framework of scientific spirituality grounded in his interpretation of Sāṅkhya and Yoga. He discusses the relationship between body, mind, and self, the practice of Pātañjala Upāsanā, and questions about consciousness that he invites scientists and scholars to examine.

The document includes diagrams of the mind and proposed paths of inquiry, a tentative plan of action, and an appendix outlining a practice of Pātañjala Upāsanā in English and Hindi. These are the author's interpretations and proposals for study and discussion.

The complete PDF is available to read below. It was [originally published by the Center for Inner Sciences](https://www.centerforinnersciences.org/scientific_spirituality.html), a separate organization co-founded by Dr. Chandra. Its mission statement belongs to that organization and does not replace the Sankhya Yog Research Center's own mission.
