The Sankhya Yog Research Center is a place to read and share work on Sāṅkhya, Yoga Darśana, Pātañjala Upāsanā, and related Vedic thought. We are beginning with an open digital home for articles and research papers.

## A place for careful study

The center's mission draws inspiration from Swami Dayanand Saraswati and the scholarly work of Dr. Harish Chandra. We hope to make serious study more accessible: readers should be able to encounter an idea, follow its sources, and consider its interpretation for themselves.

This article introduces the center. It is not a research paper. Papers will appear in the research archive as they are prepared for publication.

## What you will find here

The articles section offers writing intended for reading on the web. The papers section will provide PDF publications, with space for abstracts, authorship, and references. Both formats belong in a growing conversation about philosophy, practice, and interpretation.
